🍲 The Concept

We believe the best way to see a city is through its stomach and its people. Every day at noon, we gather the "Tribes" from various hostels across Panama City to head to Via Argentina. This is where the locals eat, and it's where we host our massive group lunch at our favorite traditional Fonda.

🤝 The Vibe: Meet Everyone

Even if you're traveling solo or staying at a smaller hostel, this is where you connect. You’ll be joining travelers coming straight from the Casco tour, plus newcomers who just arrived in the city. It’s loud, it’s social, and it’s the easiest way to find your crew for the rest of your trip.

🍱 The Deal: $12.00 All-In

We make it easy so you don't have to worry about the bill or the logistics:

Metro Ticket Included: We provide your transport to the Via Argentina area.

The "Fonda" Feast: A full, traditional Panamanian meal (Protein, Rice & Beans, Salad/Plantain).

Specialty Coffee: Finish the meal with a high-quality local brew to keep you going for the afternoon.

💡 Why Join?

If you're looking for recommendations for the rest of your week, or someone to split a taxi to the Canal later, this is where it happens. Eat alone in your hostel or join the family!

⚠️P.S. This lunch is also your "scouting mission" for the best alternative to the loud, touristy nightlife of Casco. Via Argentina Avenue at night is a whole different beast—think movie-like lighting, street artists, live alternative rock, jazz bars, and high-end cocktail spots.

⚠️Whether you want a local brewery, a rum bar, a proper PUB (shout out to the Brits!), or even a hidden TECHNO CLUB, this street has it all. Don’t miss seeing this area during the day so you know the layout before coming back later with us. You definitely should—it's way cheaper than Casco and ten times more authentic.
