Skip the gym. Feel the breeze. Own the sunset.

A community-focused session designed to break barriers and build strength.

🧱 The Experience: Why train in a box?

If you're reading this, you probably already work out. You likely have a gym membership back home (or even here in Panama). But let’s be honest: you didn't come to Panama to be trapped inside a room with recycled air-conditioning, deafening speakers, and a room full of antisocial people staring at their phones.

The point of this session isn't to "teach" you calisthenics or convert you into an athlete. The purpose is to show you just how NICE it is to move your body outdoors.

Whether you are a complete beginner or an advanced gym-goer, this is your invitation to break your routine. Here, we feel the salt air on our skin. We talk, we laugh, and we share a vibe that no indoor gym can replicate.

✨ Why You Shouldn't Skip This

The "Anti-Gym" Vibe: No walls, no bad music, and no social barriers. Just the ocean breeze and a community of women.

The Sunset: We time this perfectly. You will leave with Panama’s most beautiful memory: training as the sky turns orange over the Pacific.

Beat the Rush Hour: This happens during Panama's peak traffic. Don't waste your precious travel time sitting in a bus or an Uber.

Walkable: Located at Cinta Costera, we are walking distance from all major hostels. No taxi needed.

Certified Coaching: Guided by "Carnal" (Certified Trainer) and our Lead Female Certified Trainer, ensuring that whether you're doing your first squat or an advanced muscle-up, you're in safe hands.

📋 Logistics

For Everyone: Scalable workouts for beginners and gym veterans alike.

Time: 5:30 PM – 7:00 PM

Price: $12.00 P/P

Space: Strictly Women-Only

Group Size: Minimum 4 Persons

What to bring: Comfortable activewear and water.

⚠️ A Final Note: If you already workout, take advantage of the short time you have in this beautiful city. PLEASE skip the gym for one day and come train at the park. Give it a try—we promise you won't regret trading the treadmill for the skyline sunset.
