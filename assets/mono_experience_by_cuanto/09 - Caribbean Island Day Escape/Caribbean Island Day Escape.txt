Get ready for an early departure from your hostel in Panama City! 🚌 We'll start our journey with a scenic 1.5-hour ride through amazing tropical landscapes to the province of Colón.

Our first stop is the historic town of Portobello. 🏰 Did you know this was once one of the most important Spanish ports in the Americas, where a lot of gold was stored, and pirates like Sir Francis Drake and Henry Morgan often attacked the fortresses to get a hold of it? 🏴‍☠️

We'll have a quick snack break here, enjoying some local favorites like empanadas and “plantintart”, paired with delicious Panama-Specialty-Coffee. ☕️🥟

After our history and coffee fix, we'll head to La Guaira Port for a quick 5-minute boat ride that will take us to the beautiful Isla Grande. 🚤 Once there, we'll head to a private home with an ocean view, which will serve as our special base camp or meeting point for the day. 🏠

This is the perfect spot to hang out, chill, read, and most importantly, use a very clean bathroom and showers to freshen up throughout the day. Feel free to get lost on the island and chat with the locals. 🤙

You'll have plenty of activities to choose from:

- Relax by the beach and take a warm ocean bath. 🏖️

- Hike up to the abandoned lighthouse viewpoint for an incredible sunset & parrot watching. 🦜📸

Enjoy a delicious local lunch at one of the island's restaurants. 🥗 (5-15 USD) We recommend packing a lunch as well. Vegan options available

Our boat will pick us up around 3:00 PM for the return trip, ensuring we get back to Panama City feeling completely refreshed and full of Caribbean memories for sunset hours. 🌅
