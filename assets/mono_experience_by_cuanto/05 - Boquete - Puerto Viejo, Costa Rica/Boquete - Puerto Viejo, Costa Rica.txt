You’ve hiked the volcanoes, you’ve seen the waterfalls, and you’ve lived your absolute best life in Panama. Now, it’s time to cross the border into Costa Rica. You’re heading to Puerto Viejo, where the vibes are high and the prices are… well, twice as high. We love our neighbors, but your wallet might miss us. Enjoy the ride, say hi to a new beginning, and try not to cry when you see the price of a beer across the border. 🇨🇷💸

The Journey:

The Route: We take the stunning road trip through the Continental Divide, heading toward the Caribbean side. You’ll see the lush transition from the Chiriquí highlands back down to the coast of Bocas del Toro before hitting the land border. 🌴⛰️

The Frontier (Sixaola): Get ready for the most legendary logistics system on the planet. These guys have been using the same paperwork and filing system since Panama was part of Great Colombia. It’s a vintage experience! 📄🕰️

The "Latino Luck": A word of advice: if you look like one of us locals, prepare for the "extended" wait. If you’re a tourist girl, you might get waved through like royalty. For the rest of us? Good luck making those lines and filling out papers that look like they belong in a museum. (We’re kidding... mostly. Take it with humor!) 🧔‍♂️🛂

The Transition:

Panama: The country that stole your heart (and was kind to your bank account).

Costa Rica: The land of sloths, surf, and $15 salads.

The Hand-off: We’ll guide you through the chaos of the Sixaola bridge. Once you cross that old railway bridge over the river, you’re officially in the land of Pura Vida. 🌉👣

The Mono Solo Farewell:
Thank you so much for choosing Panama as your destination. We genuinely love our travelers and our Costa Rican neighbors. We’re happy to help you start your next chapter, even if it means leaving us. Just remember: you can always stay a few more days in Panama if the border line looks too long! 🇵🇦❤️

📝 Mono Solo Logistics Note:
The Paperwork: Keep your passport handy, your exit tax receipt ready, and your patience at maximum levels. 📑🧘‍♂️

The Bridge: You’ll likely have to walk across the bridge at Sixaola with your luggage. It’s a rite of passage. Embrace the "adventure traveler" look. 🧳💪

Currency: Make sure you have some cash for the exit fees. The border doesn't accept "vibes" or "One Love" as payment. 💵🚫
