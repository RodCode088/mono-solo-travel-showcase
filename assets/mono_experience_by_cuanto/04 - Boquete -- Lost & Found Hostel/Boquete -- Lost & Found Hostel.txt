Let’s be honest with each other: you’re going to Lost & Found because you want the jungle experience... or because every bed in Boquete and Bocas was booked solid and this was the only dot left on the map. Either way, you’re in for a treat. This isn't a door-to-door service; it’s a drop-off at the edge of civilization. 🌲👣

The Logistics:

Departure: 8:00 AM sharp from Boquete.

The Drop-off: The shuttle will leave you at a specific spot on the side of the highway. From there, your real journey begins: a hike up the mountain to reach the hostel. If you brought a 50lb suitcase, this is the moment you’ll regret every single souvenir you bought. 🧳📈

The Route: You’re heading into the Fortuna Nature Reserve. It’s one of the wettest and most biodiverse spots in the country. 🌧️🦋

The Vibe:
The drive is short—usually around an hour—but the transition is massive. You’ll leave the coffee farms behind and enter a world of moss, ferns, and incredible views. Since the shuttle is already on its way to Bocas, you’re basically hitching a professional ride to the halfway point. 🌬️⛰️

The Mono Solo Reality Check:
We know why you’re going there. Whether it’s for the famous treasure hunt or just because you needed a place to sleep, we’ll get you to the trailhead in one piece. Just remember: once the van pulls away, it’s just you and the uphill climb. Enjoy the burn! 💨🧗‍♀️

📝 Mono Solo Logistics Note:
The Hike: This shuttle drops you at the bottom. You have to walk up to the hostel. It’s a real hike. Don't say I didn't warn you. ⚠️

Supplies: There are no supermarkets where you're going. Grab your snacks and essentials in Boquete before the 8:00 AM departure. 🍫🧴

The Weather: It will rain. Keep your rain cover handy for your backpack. ☔
