So, you’ve survived the Santa Catalina surf. You’re sunburnt, you’re exhausted, and you’ve realized that getting out of this town using public transport is like trying to solve a Rubik’s cube while blindfolded. You could spend your afternoon waiting for the one bus that might or might not show up to take you to Soná... or you can hop in with us and be back in Boquete in time for a late dinner. 🏄‍♂️➡️🏔️

The Logistics:

Pick-up Time: 2:00 PM. This gives you one last morning to grab a session at La Punta or a quick breakfast at the bakery before we head out.

The Team: Just like the way down, we need a minimum of 6 people to make this happen. Round up the crew from the hostel—tell them the mountain air is calling. 🗣️🚐

The Route: We’re leaving the coast of Veraguas and climbing back up into the Chiriquí Highlands.

The Experience:
This is the "Nap Shuttle." Most of you will be passed out before we even hit the Pan-American Highway. While the local buses are stopping at every single corner to pick up someone’s grandmother and a crate of chickens, we’re cruising straight through. We’ll get you back to Boquete rested, so you’re ready to trade your surfboard for hiking boots. 😴🥾

The Mono Solo Reality Check:
We know you’re sad to leave the beach, but let’s be honest: your skin needs a break from the sun, and your liver needs a break from the beach bars. We’ll get you back to the "Best Vida" in the mountains without the headache of the Santiago terminal transfer. 🍻🚫

📝 Mono Solo Logistics Note:
The "Surf Fatigue": If you’re feeling a bit "ocean-sick" from the morning session, grab some ginger tea or crackers before we leave at 2:00 PM. 🤢🍪

Drop-off: We’ll drop you right back in the center of Boquete. Easy access to your next hostel or the nearest pharmacy for more aloe vera. 🏨🧴

The Connection: Once you’re back, don’t forget to check our schedule for the Barú Sunrise Hike. If you thought the Pacific waves were big, wait until you see the view from the highest point in Panama. 🌋✨
