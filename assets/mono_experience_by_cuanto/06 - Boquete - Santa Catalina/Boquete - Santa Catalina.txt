Descripcion completa:
--------------------
Congratulations! You just realized that Santa Catalina is essentially on its own planet. It’s "one thousand hours away" from everything else. You could take the "Authentic Local Experience"—which involves 3 to 4 different buses, several terminals, and a full day of choices. I’ve done it; it’s a character-building fun adventure. But if you'd rather arrive with your sanity intact, we’re here to help. 🏄‍♂️🚌

The Logistics:

Pick-up Window: Between 7:45 AM and 8:45 AM.

The Mission: We’re pulling you out of the Boquete mountains and dropping you directly at the Pacific surf hub.

The Requirement: This is a team effort. We need a minimum of 6 people to make this run. So, go find some friends in the hostel lobby and convince them that they need to see the sunset at La Punta. 🌅👥

The Experience:
While the public bus travelers are currently sweating in a terminal in Santiago, you’ll be relaxing in a van with people who actually have interesting stories to tell. We’ll get you there rested and hydrated, which is important because you’re going to need that energy for all the wipeouts you’re about to receive while surfing or the nitrogen you’ll be breathing while scuba diving at Coiba. 🤿🌊

The Mono Solo Reality Check:
We aren't just giving you a ride; we're giving you an extra half-day of vacation that you would have otherwise spent staring at a bus schedule in Spanish. Use that time wisely—preferably with a cold beer and your feet in the black sand. 🍺🏝️

📝 Mono Solo Logistics Note:
Group Effort: If you don't have 6 people yet, let us know. We’ll keep a list and try to match you with other "stranded" travelers. 📋

The Long Road: It’s still a drive, but it’s a scenic one. We’ll pass through the heart of Panama's cattle country and the rolling hills of Veraguas. 🐄🤠

The Arrival: We drop you right in the village. From there, it’s a short walk to the waves. 👣
