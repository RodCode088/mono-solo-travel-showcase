You’ve spent enough time in Bocas to forget what shoes feel like. Now, it’s time to head back to the highlands. We’re doing the reverse route, starting with a boat and ending with a climb into the cloud forest. Just remember: you are leaving a place where time is a suggestion, not a rule. 🌴🚤

The Logistics:

The Departure: Be at the Isla Colón port by 11:30 AM.

The "Caribbean Watch": In Bocas, people don't wear watches; they just "feel" the time. If the boat captain is five minutes late, he’s not being rude—he’s just gifting you five more minutes of paradise. 🥥🕰️

The Transit: Your $30 gets you the boat ride to Almirante and the van ride back up to Boquete. You’ll be swapping the saltwater for the "Bajareque" (Boquete mist) by late afternoon.

The Mono Solo Reality Check:
We love you all, and we’re all about that One Love Marley vibe. But let’s be real: you’re paying for transportation, not a teleportation device. If you’re in a massive rush to get back to a schedule, buy a plane ticket. Otherwise, sit back, enjoy the bumpy ride through the mountains, and embrace the chaos. 🚐💨

Special Note for our German Friends:
If the lack of punctuality is giving you a minor existential crisis, feel free to save your complaints until you’re back in Germany. We say this with love, knowing you’re probably the only ones actually standing at the dock at 11:29 AM. 😉🇩🇪

📝 Mono Solo Logistics Note:
Check-in: Don't be "Caribbean Late" yourself. The boat won't wait for you, even if the captain was late picking up the previous group. ⚓

Luggage: Keep your valuables in a waterproof bag. The crossing to Almirante can get a bit "splashy" depending on the wind. 🌊🎒

The Route: You’ll be heading back through the Devil’s Chin. It’s just as curvy on the way up, so keep the ginger ale handy if you get car-sick. 🤢⛰️
