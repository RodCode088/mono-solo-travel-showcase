📝 The Vibe

Look, I’m not taking you on a public bus to sit in traffic with fifty other tourists. We’re doing this properly. We leave the city behind and head straight to the clouds.

We start the day at Lotus Wellness. This isn’t just a "stop"—it’s a ritual. My close friend owns this spot, and it’s perched so high you can practically see the curvature of the earth. We’re going to sit on that terrace, sip on some serious specialty Panama coffee or organic herbal teas, and for the smokers, light up some quality artisanal tobacco.

It’s the moment of clarity. You’ll be looking directly across at the peak of Chame Hill—the very one we’re about to conquer—realizing that life is actually pretty fucking good.

📍 What We’re Actually Doing

Once we’re properly caffeinated and the vibes are calibrated, we hit the trail. Chame Hill doesn't care about your feelings; it’s a steady, honest incline. It’s going to make you sweat, you might curse my name once or twice, but the 360-degree view at the summit—mangroves on one side, the Pacific on the other—is worth every drop of effort.

After we’ve dominated the mountain, for an extra $10 we can drop back down to sea level. We head to the coast for a real-deal Panamanian beach. Good food and the ocean breeze to wash off the mountain dust.
