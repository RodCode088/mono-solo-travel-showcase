We don’t do Taboga like everyone else. Forget the crowded public ferry and the generic tourist terminals. We head across the Panama Canal to Veracruz—a gritty, authentic local beach completely skipped by traditional travel agencies. From there, we jump onto a private speedboat captained by a Taboga local to cross the ocean.

This is a raw exploration experience dictated by nature. Because the Pacific Ocean's tide rises and drops by more than 4 meters, the ocean decides exactly how many hours of island sanctuary we get. Once on the island, you are free to hike wild jungle trails, hit viewpoint peaks looking back at the city skyline, or chill on the sand.
